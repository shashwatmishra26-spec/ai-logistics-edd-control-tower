Primary Objective — Prevent EDD Breaches In-Transit & Recover Failed Deliveries

Everything in this section is a work queue, not a chart: which lanes are about to miss their promised delivery date while the shipment is still moving (and the customer-care + push alerts already queued for them), which lanes need their EDD promise padded because they structurally can't hit it, and which failed deliveries (NDR) need a call to get a landmark, address confirmation, or alternate phone number. Trend lines and scorecards behind these numbers are further down the page, under Supporting Analytics.

Deliveries That Could Not Be Completed — NDR Consolidation & Outreach

For every shipment with an unresolved failed-delivery (NDR) event: a consolidated view, a mock care-team digest email, a PII-safe IVR call sheet, and mock customer push/email outreach — all asking for the same three things: a landmark, an address confirmation, or an alternate phone number. See the privacy note in src/ndr_agent/ndr_consolidated_report.py — no real name/phone/address is ever used.

NDR Consolidated Report

IVR Call Sheet — Outbound Calling Queue

PII-safe: contact details are resolved via contact_lookup_key against a secure CRM at call time and are never stored in this report. Full sheet: outputs/ivr_call_sheet.csv.

Customer Outreach — Push & Email Queue (MOCK)

Customer-facing push notification and email copy asking directly for the missing information. No real message is sent.

Data confidence: ACTUAL fields are taken as-is from the source workbook. DERIVED fields are computed via arithmetic/date logic on ACTUAL fields (e.g. transit days, EDD miss flag). SYNTHETIC fields (carrier, AWB, hub, distance, lane class, SLA targets) do not exist in the source data and are industry-informed assumptions, fully documented in docs/data_assumptions.md. AI_PREDICTED fields are outputs of the trained ML models in src/models/. PROJECTED/SIMULATED values in the intervention simulator are forward-looking scenarios, never a restatement of historical performance.
